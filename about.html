About me

I'm Matthew Nothdurft, a licensed real estate broker and the founder of MJN Realty, based in the Adirondack region of New York.

I grew up on the shores of Schroon Lake, kindergarten through twelfth grade, with the mountains for a backyard and the lake for a playground. College took me just down the road to Queensbury, and work carried me farther, to other towns and other lives. But this lake has a way of calling you home. Now I’m back, swimming and boating the same clear water and still finding something new around every bend.
